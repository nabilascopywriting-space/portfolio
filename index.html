About

Who am I?

My name is Nab-eela. I'm an email copywriter by day, fiction author by night.

After getting coached by Brandon Storey, a Kelowna native who has generated over $1M for his clients and written for Beehiiv itself, I have learned all the tricks of the trade of how to be a not-so-awful copywriter.

Writing fiction has also helped me A LOT in this regard. After 8 years of telling stories and studying human psychology, I know what makes people tick and keeps them hooked into reading my stories.

"Storytelling is the secret sauce of some of the biggest brands today. Nike. Apple. Airbnb. It's what gives people a reason to stay, by forging emotional bonds that make them FEEL something. And I'm here to help you do just that."

I write high-converting copy for clients from all industries but particularly enjoy working with service-based and e-commerce businesses solving real, tangible problems.
